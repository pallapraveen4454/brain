Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my quiz progress or cloud sync?

Sign in with your registered Google account or Email in the Profile or Settings screen to sync all your XP, coins, streaks, and unlocked badges automatically from Google Cloud Firestore.

How do Daily Challenge streaks work?

Complete at least one Daily Challenge every 24 hours to increase your streak counter. Streaks refresh daily at 00:00 UTC.

How do I request account deletion?

You can delete your account instantly in the app under Settings > Privacy & Security > Delete Account or submit a request on our Account Deletion Page.
