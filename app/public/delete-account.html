⚠️ Important Warning Regarding Account Deletion

Deleting your BrainQuizAI account is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, all your accumulated Experience Points (XP), virtual coins, daily challenge streaks, earned badges, category mastery stats, and leaderboard rankings cannot be restored.

1. Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving and confirming an account deletion request, the following data is permanently purged from Google Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore:

  • Authentication Profile: Your Firebase User ID (UID), registered email address, authentication timestamps, and display name.
  • Gameplay & Progress Statistics: All recorded XP, level progress, virtual coins balance, historical quiz logs, and daily challenge streak counts.
  • Achievements & Custom Content: Unlocked achievement badges and AI custom quiz history.
  • Leaderboard Entries: Your user profile entry is permanently removed from all global and category leaderboards.

2. Data Retention Notice

We do not retain personal user information after account deletion. Non-identifying crash logs or aggregated operational metrics that cannot be linked to any user may be retained for temporary technical diagnostics as permitted by law.

3. How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

You can instantly delete your account directly from your Android device:

  1. Open the BrainQuizAI application on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen (gear icon in the bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice in the confirmation dialog.
