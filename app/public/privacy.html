Overview: BrainQuizAI ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how we process and protect your data, and your rights under Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

1. Information We Collect

When using BrainQuizAI, we collect minimal data necessary to deliver a synchronized, secure, and engaging quiz experience:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you register or sign in using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we store your unique Firebase User ID (UID), email address, and optional display name through Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Your quiz score, Experience Points (XP), coins, current streak count, daily challenge completion history, earned achievements, category mastery, and leaderboard standings are synced to Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Local On-Device Data: The application uses an on-device local database (Room Database) and Android Encrypted Preferences to store question packs offline, cache gameplay progress, sound/haptic preferences, and theme choices for seamless offline play.
  • AI Prompt Interactions: When using the AI Quiz Generator, your chosen topic prompt (e.g., "Quantum Physics" or "World Capitals") is processed via Google Gemini API to generate trivia questions. No personal identifying information is attached to AI prompt requests.
  • Device & Diagnostic Telemetry: We collect non-identifiable diagnostic metrics, app performance data, and crash reports to maintain application stability and performance across Android devices.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We partner with trusted industry-standard service providers who operate under strict privacy compliance:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages secure user authentication, user sessions, cloud backups, and real-time database synchronization. View Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API: Generates custom dynamic quiz content on-demand. Quiz prompts are processed securely and are not used to identify individual users. View Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: Serves non-intrusive advertisements and optional rewarded video ads (allowing players to earn extra hints or coins). AdMob may use standard advertising identifiers according to Google's advertising policies. View Google Ads Privacy.

3. Notifications

BrainQuizAI may send optional push notifications on Android for:

  • Daily Challenge reminders and streak maintenance notifications.
  • Quiz score updates or achievement unlocks.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time directly in your Android system settings or in the app's Settings menu.

4. Data Security & Storage

Your data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols and encrypted at rest in Google Cloud Firestore. We maintain strict security rules ensuring that only authenticated users can access or modify their own gameplay statistics.

5. Account & Data Deletion

We respect your right to be forgotten. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time through either of the following methods:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Delete Account inside the BrainQuizAI app and confirm deletion. All cloud data (Firebase Auth profile, Firestore user documents, streaks, leaderboard entries, and quiz progress) will be permanently purged immediately.
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page or email your request directly to pallapraveen4454@gmail.com. Web deletion requests are processed within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

BrainQuizAI is an educational trivia and general knowledge game designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for immediate deletion.
